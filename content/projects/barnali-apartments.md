Ten flats on a Garfa plot where an old single-storey house stood until last year.
Casting is at third-floor level. Brickwork on the lower floors has started.

Buying at this stage means you can still watch how the building is being put
together, which is really the argument for doing it. Ask to be shown the
reinforcement before the next slab goes in.
