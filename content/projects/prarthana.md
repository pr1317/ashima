The first building Ashima Engineering finished. It went up on a Santoshpur plot
belonging to a family who decided to develop rather than sell, and it is named
after the founder's daughter.

Eight flats over three floors. It set the pattern for everything that came
after, joint-venture terms, a small building, and the owner's share written
down before anything was signed.
