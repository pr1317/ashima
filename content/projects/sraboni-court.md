Fourteen flats over four floors on a plot off Mukundapur Main Road, developed on
joint-venture terms with the family who have owned the land since the nineteen
sixties. Two and three bedroom flats, five still unsold.

It is the kind of four-storey building we have been putting up in these
localities for thirty years. No podium and no clubhouse. Covered parking at
ground level, and a lift that reaches every floor.

Come and see the site before you decide. Somebody is there most mornings.
