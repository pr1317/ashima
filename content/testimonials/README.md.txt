Real Google reviews only.

To add one, create a file here called something like `sunita-garfa.md`:

    ---
    firstName: Sunita
    context: Garfa, 2019
    quote: "The date they gave us for possession was the date we moved in."
    sourceUrl: https://g.page/r/YOUR-REVIEW-LINK
    ---

The homepage section renders only when there are three or more. Below that it
does not appear at all. Do not write these; copy them from the Google Business
Profile, attributed by first name and locality.
